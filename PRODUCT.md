# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creators and founders evaluating Catra as a builder, facilitator, or advisor. They need fast evidence of judgment, relevant work, and engagement fit.

## Product Purpose

Present Catra's point of view and prove his ability through shipped work. Success means a qualified visitor understands the work, trusts the proof, and can start a useful conversation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, mechanical, and opinionated. The experience should feel precise without becoming clinical, and personal without becoming decorative.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a project index, generic technical portfolio, editorial AI template, SaaS landing page, or crypto neon interface. Avoid ornamental effects, invented metrics, repeated card grids, and unexplained jargon.

## Design Principles

1. Show proof before asking for trust.
2. Practice “less, but better” through useful reduction.
3. Make ownership a visible point of view, not a slogan.
4. Prefer honest artifacts and direct language over claims.
5. Give one clear path from interest to conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, browser zoom, and 44px touch targets.

## Keeping work current

Essays update themselves. `scripts/update_feed.py` runs daily and rewrites only the
block between `<!-- FEED:START -->` and `<!-- FEED:END -->`.

Projects do not. Every project link in `index.html` is hand written, so the list
drifts behind the work. Two rules keep it honest:

1. Ship a project, then add it to `index.html` in the same week.
2. Run `git pull` before editing `index.html`. The feed bot commits to the same file.

List a project only when a stranger can open it. No private repo, no dead link.
